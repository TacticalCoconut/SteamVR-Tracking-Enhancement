# Code of conduct

This project is a place to make VR tracking better. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull
requests and discussions — is expected to keep it a place where that work is possible.

## Expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith. Most unclear reports are unclear, not hostile.
- Be specific and honest about what you tested and what you did not.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that they should say why.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including what is in logs they attached.
- Persistent off-topic or bad-faith posting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep
breaking them. To report a problem, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
